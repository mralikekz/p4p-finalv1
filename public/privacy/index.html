Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

At P4P, your privacy is our absolute priority. This document explains in a simple, transparent, and secure way how we collect and process information.

1. Information Collection

We respect the decentralized nature of Web3. P4P does NOT collect any sensitive or traditional personal data, such as your passwords, real names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

  • Pi Network Data: Through the official Pi Network SDK, we only access your basic public identifiers: your public Pi username and unique User ID.
  • Transaction Details: We collect transaction data, including the payment ID and transaction hash (TXID), exclusively to verify, validate, and count your casted votes in the athlete rankings.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

  • Blockchain Validation: Your transaction and payment data are shared solely with the Pi Network blockchain to verify authenticity.
  • No Commercial Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease your information to third-party advertising networks or external services. Your data is used strictly for in-app utility.
